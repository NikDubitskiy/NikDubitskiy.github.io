How I came to this topic

At MIREA, I studied Nanotechnology and Microsystem Engineering. My early conference work concerned liquid crystals and microplastic detection. I then moved to oxide films, X-ray diffraction and electronic transport. During my master’s degree at HSE University, this developed into a study of substrate effects in SrIrO₃.
