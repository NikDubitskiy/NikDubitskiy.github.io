Strontium iridates

SrIrO₃

3D network · semimetal

IrO₆ octahedra connect in three dimensions. Both electron and hole states contribute to transport.

Sr₂IrO₄

Layered structure · antiferromagnet

Octahedral layers are separated by SrO layers. Spin–orbit coupling and electron correlations produce a Mott state.

Schematics show octahedral connectivity and contrasting electronic states. Octahedral rotations and Sr atoms are omitted; E(k) curves are illustrative.

I study how film thickness, substrate and growth conditions affect these materials. My work on SrIrO₃ concerns electronic and magnetotransport; on Sr₂IrO₄, conduction mechanisms and Josephson structures.

Why it matters for spintronics

Spintronic devices store information in magnetic states and use electric currents to write or read them. Iridates offer strong coupling between spin and orbital motion, with experimental devices already demonstrating its uses.

Current-driven magnetic switching

In a SrIrO₃/SrRuO₃ bilayer, current in the iridate generates a spin–orbit torque that switches the neighbouring magnetic layer at low temperature. This mechanism is being explored for magnetic memory.

Liu et al. · Nature Nanotechnology, 2019

Antiferromagnetic memory

Sr₂IrO₄ with 1% Ga supports two resistance states that remain after the magnetic field is removed. Writing and readout were demonstrated at 10 K.

Wang et al. · Nature Communications, 2019
