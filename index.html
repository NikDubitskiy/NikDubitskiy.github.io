How does a substrate change electron transport in a thin film?

I study the structure of strontium iridate thin films and how electric current flows through them. I am interested in connecting what we change during growth to what we measure afterwards. Why does one substrate give a metallic resistance trend while another changes the low-temperature response?

Research

One material. Five substrates.

In my master’s thesis, I compared approximately 35 nm SrIrO₃ films on five single-crystal substrates. Structure, surface, chemical-state and resistance measurements help separate the different effects of the substrate.

Resistance was measured from 4.2 to 300 K; the Hall effect at 2 and 10 K in fields up to ±7 T.
