Josephson Transport through Spin–Orbit-Coupled SrIrO₃ and Sr₂IrO₄ Interlayers

N. Dubitskiy, G. Ovsyannikov, Y. Kislinskii, G. Ulev, A. Petrzhik

Mesoscience & Nanotechnology · 2026 · 1(3), 01-03005

About this work

We studied Nb/iridate/YBa₂Cu₃O₇₋δ superconducting structures with two interlayer materials. Both conducting SrIrO₃ and insulating Sr₂IrO₄ carried a Josephson current. Their different transport regimes were reflected in normal resistance and characteristic voltage.

Research question

How does replacing a conducting interlayer with an insulating one change a superconducting junction?

Measurements

Current–voltage characteristics and magnetic-field-dependent critical current were measured at 4.2 K.

Results and context

IсRₙ was 0.1–0.3 μV for 15-nm SrIrO₃ and 41–79 μV for 5-nm Sr₂IrO₄. The publication gives the full results and comparison conditions.
