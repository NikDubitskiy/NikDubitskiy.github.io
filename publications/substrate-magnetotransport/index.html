Nikita Dubitskiy and colleagues

Substrate-dependent structure and magnetotransport of epitaxial SrIrO₃ thin films and SrIrO₃/La₀.₇Sr₀.₃MnO₃ heterostructures

G. A. Ovsyannikov, N. V. Dubitskiy, G. D. Ulev, K. I. Constantinian, I. E. Moskal, V. A. Baydikova, A. M. Petrzhik, A. V. Shadrin, A. V. Mashirov

Journal of Physics and Chemistry of Solids · 2027 · 220, 114155

About this work

We compare the structure of SrIrO₃ films with their electronic and magnetic transport. Changing the substrate changes the conditions for epitaxial growth and lattice strain. A second part concerns SrIrO₃/La₀.₇Sr₀.₃MnO₃ heterostructures, which combine the iridate with a magnetic oxide.

Research question

How does substrate choice relate to film structure and its response to a magnetic field?

Measurements

X-ray diffraction, photoelectron spectroscopy, resistance, Hall-effect and magnetoresistance measurements.

Results and context

The work connects individual oxide films with heterostructures relevant to spintronics.
