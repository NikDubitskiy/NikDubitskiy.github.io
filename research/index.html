Research

SrIrO₃ and Sr₂IrO₄ thin films, electronic transport and heterostructures.

Research areas

SrIrO₃: growth and electronic transport

My SrIrO₃ research examines how substrate, thickness and deposition conditions affect film structure and conduction. I relate X-ray measurements to resistance, magnetoresistance and the Hall effect. The five-substrate series in my master’s thesis is one part of this work.

Related paper

Sr₂IrO₄: structure and conduction mechanisms

For Sr₂IrO₄ films, we compared deposition methods and investigated temperature-dependent conduction. These studies address thermally activated and hopping transport and their relationship to sample structure.

Related paper

Iridates in superconducting heterostructures

In collaborative studies, we investigate current transport through SrIrO₃ and Sr₂IrO₄ interlayers in Josephson structures. Barrier properties, thickness and interfaces with superconducting electrodes are central to this work.
