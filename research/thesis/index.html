Substrate-Driven Epitaxial Strain Effects on Structure and Electronic Transport in Spin–Orbit-Coupled SrIrO₃ Thin Films

Nikita Dubitskiy

Master’s thesis · HSE University · 2026

About this work

My master’s thesis examines SrIrO₃ films grown by RF magnetron sputtering on five substrates: SrTiO₃, NdGaO₃, LSAT, LaAlO₃ and PMN–PT. Comparing one film series connects substrate choice with structure, local chemical environment and electrical properties.

Research question

How do lattice strain, local chemistry and surface morphology relate to transport in an epitaxial film?

Measurements

X-ray diffraction and AFM probe structure and surface morphology; XPS examines chemical state. Resistance was measured from 4.2 to 300 K, with Hall measurements at 2 and 10 K in fields up to ±7 T.

Results and context

A separate experiment uses a piezoelectric PMN–PT substrate: an applied voltage changes substrate strain while the electrical response is measured in the SrIrO₃ film. The thesis is one part of my broader work on SrIrO₃, Sr₂IrO₄ and oxide heterostructures.
